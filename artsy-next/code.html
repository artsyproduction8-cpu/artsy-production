FREQUENTLY ENCOUNTERED QUESTIONS

WHAT IS ARTSY? +

Artsy Production is a curated two-sided post-production studio and technological platform pairing high-growth brands, creators, and cinema productions with elite, verified video and photo editors under transparent productized pricing.

WHAT IS THE DELIVERY TIMELINE? +

Standard delivery ranges from 7 to 10 calendar days depending on project footage volume and camera complexity. For urgent brand, store, and UGC cuts, an expedited 48–72h rush SLA is available with an immutable deadline guarantee.

HOW DO YOU ENSURE QUALITY? +

All editors undergo strict portfolio vetting and admin approval. Every cut passes through in-house senior QC before client delivery, supported by an integrated frame-accurate review player allowing timestamped comments and two complimentary revision rounds.

WHAT IS YOUR REFUND POLICY? +

Pre-production cancellations receive a full 100% refund. Once editing has commenced, refunds are scaled transparently to project progress and logged as compensatory ledger entries under administrator review.

WHO CAN BOOK SERVICES ON ARTSY? +

Artsy serves creative directors, marketing agencies, wedding cinematographers, e-commerce stores, brand founders, and corporate enterprise teams seeking turnkey, studio-grade post-production without hiring overhead.

CAN I BOOK THE SAME FREELANCER AGAIN? +

Yes. When placing repeat briefs or recurring catalog orders, you can specify your preferred editor or in-house fellow, and our intelligent matching engine prioritizes their schedule and direct assignment.
